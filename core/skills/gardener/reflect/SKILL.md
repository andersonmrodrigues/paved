# Reflect
## When to use
A repeated mistake or useful correction.
## Required context
The incident and its cause.
## Preconditions
Avoid speculative policy.
## Procedure
Prefer a check, then a rule, then a reference edit; otherwise discard.
Activate `gardener` for proposals and `decisions` for architectural records.
## Tools
Available maintenance commands.
## Rules
Human approval precedes removals.
## Verification
Check that the lesson addresses the incident.
## Evidence
Concrete examples, not anecdotes.
## Completion criteria
A durable home or explicit discard.
## Failure modes
Duplicate guidance: consolidate by proposal.
